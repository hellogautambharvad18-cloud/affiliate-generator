FAQ

Common questions

How do I get access?

AffiliateGen is invite only. The owner creates an account for you with an email and password, and sets how long your access lasts.

Do I need my own AI or API keys?

No. Content generation runs on the owner's keys, so you only need to log in and fill in the product details.

What do I receive at the end?

A ZIP file with a complete static website. Unzip it and upload it to any web host, then point your domain at it.

Can I edit the generated site?

Yes. The output is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so you can change any text, image or link after downloading.

Is the content ready to publish as is?

Treat it as a strong first draft. Always verify product claims, pricing, guarantees, testimonials, certifications and image usage rights before going live.
